Before you call

Questions we hear every week.

How fast can someone get here?

For emergencies like a burst pipe or no water, the dispatcher gives you an arrival window on the call, usually within the hour. Routine jobs are booked same day or next day.

Do I get a price before work starts?

Yes. Your tech diagnoses the problem, gives you a flat price in writing, and starts only after you approve it.

Can one visit cover plumbing and handyman work?

Yes. List everything when you book and we send a tech who can handle the pipes and the punch list in the same trip, with no second trip fee.

Should I shut off the water?

If water is spreading, turn the main shut-off clockwise until it stops. It is usually where the water line enters the house, near the meter or the water heater.
