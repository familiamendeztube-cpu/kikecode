Before you call

Questions we hear every week

How fast can someone get here?

For emergencies like a burst pipe or no water, the dispatcher gives you an arrival window on the call. Routine jobs are usually booked same day or next day.

Do I get a price before work starts?

Yes. The tech looks at the problem, gives you a flat price, and starts only after you approve it. No hourly surprises.

Can one visit cover plumbing and handyman work?

That is the point of the crew. List everything when you book, and we send someone who can handle the pipes and the punch list in the same trip.

Should I shut off the water?

If water is spreading, turn the main shut-off clockwise until it stops. It is usually where the water line enters the house, near the meter or the water heater.
